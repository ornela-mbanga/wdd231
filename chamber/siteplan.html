ORVY

Individual Project Site Plan

1. Site Name

ORVY

ORVY is the name of our brand, created by combining my name and my sister's name. As the first daughters of our parents, ORVY represents our story, our sisterhood, and the vision we share together.

We chose this name because we want ORVY to carry our identity wherever the brand grows. It represents our creativity, determination, and desire to build something meaningful together in the areas of beauty, fashion, accessories, and lifestyle.

For us, ORVY is more than a brand name. It is a way to preserve our story, represent our family, and leave our names and our mark wherever our journey takes us.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of the ORVY website is to introduce the ORVY brand and share its beauty, fashion, accessories, and lifestyle vision with visitors.

The website will showcase featured products and collections, provide beauty and lifestyle inspiration, introduce the story and vision of the brand, and provide visitors with ways to connect with ORVY through social media and contact information.

The website will serve as a digital presentation of the brand and will help visitors discover ORVY and its story.

3. Scenarios

The following questions represent information that visitors may want to find on the ORVY website:

  • What kind of beauty, fashion, accessories, and lifestyle products or collections does ORVY feature?
  • How can I learn more about the ORVY brand and its story?
  • Where can I follow ORVY on social media?
  • How can I contact ORVY for more information?

Gold

5. Typography

Playfair Display

Playfair Display will be used for the main headings, important titles, and selected brand elements. It gives the website an elegant and stylish appearance.

Roboto

Roboto will be used for paragraphs, navigation, buttons, forms, and general body text. It is simple and easy to read on both small and large screens.
